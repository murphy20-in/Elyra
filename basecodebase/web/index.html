Why we built this

Existing apps ask you to be visible before you are safe.

For a lot of people in India, a dating profile isn't a bit of fun — it's a document that can reach a family WhatsApp group, a landlord, or an employer. The apps were never designed for that stake.

Outing is one screenshot away

The core risk

One saved photo travels further than any privacy policy. Most apps treat your face as the price of entry and offer nothing between "fully public" and "not here at all".

You can't tell who's real

Trust deficit

Fake profiles, catfishing and extortion set-ups target queer users specifically. Verification is usually a blue tick that proves someone paid, not that someone is safe.

Everyone wants a different thing

Mismatched intent

Swiping flattens looking for a partner, looking for friends, and looking for something discreet into one identical gesture — then leaves you to sort out the mismatch in the chat.

How it works

Three steps, and you stay in control of all of them.

Build two selves

Your public profile is what the world sees — as much or as little as you want, photo optional. Your private profile holds the rest: real name, face, HIV status, anything you choose. It's encrypted, and it stays sealed until you say otherwise.

Say what you're here for

Pick your intent — exploring, something serious, discreet, or friendship. We match on that first, then on preferences and what you actually write about yourself. No swiping, and every match tells you why it happened.

Reveal on your terms

When trust builds, you can unlock parts of your private layer for one person, for a set time. Change your mind and it re-seals instantly. Every reveal is logged so you always know exactly who saw what.

Questions

The things worth asking before you sign up.

Do I have to upload a photo?

No. You can create a full public profile with no face at all and still match, chat and meet people. If you do verify your face, that image is used for the liveness check and stored in your private layer — it never appears publicly unless you put it there yourself.

What happens if I revoke a reveal?

The fields re-encrypt immediately and the other person loses access on their next request — there's no cached copy on their device. We log the revocation in your audit trail. What we can't do is un-see something someone already read, which is why reveals are time-bound by default.

Can Elyra staff read my private profile or my chats?

Private profile fields are encrypted and are not readable by staff in normal operation. Chat messages pass through automated moderation, and a message is only surfaced to a human reviewer when it is reported or flagged as a safety risk — with the reviewer seeing that message, not your history.

Is my data stored outside India?

No. Primary data stays on Indian infrastructure, and our handling is aligned to the DPDP Act. We don't sell data, we don't run ads against your profile, and we don't share your presence on Elyra with anyone.

How do I delete my account?

One tap in settings. Your profiles, matches, messages and embeddings are purged, and the encryption keys for your private layer are destroyed — which makes anything in backups permanently unreadable. No retention period, no reactivation window.

Who is Elyra for?

Anyone in India's LGBTQIA+ community aged 18 and over, whatever you're looking for — a relationship, friendship, or something you're still working out. You'll never be asked to declare more about yourself than you want to.
