## C. 2-minute pitch (investor call, intro meeting, warm intro response)

**Hook (0:00–0:15)**
> Every dating app on the planet assumes its users can be public. For tens of millions of people in India — and across MENA, SEA, and LATAM — that assumption is exactly why they don't use the product. We're building Elyra for them.

**Problem (0:15–0:35)**
> Across India, 73% of identity-sensitive users fear public exposure on swipe apps. 1 in 4 encounter fake profiles before a real conversation starts. And not a single major app builds offline safety — SOS, check-ins, emergency contacts — into the product loop. The result is a queer middle class with smartphones, willingness-to-pay, and no product that respects how they actually meet people.

**Solution (0:35–1:05)**
> Elyra is the Trust OS for modern connection. Six surfaces, one product. The *Pehchaan Layer* gives every user a public profile and an AES-256-encrypted private profile — reveal-by-consent, audit-logged, revocable. Matching is intent-based and explainable: 30% intent, 35% embedding similarity, 20% distance, 15% preference fit, all surfaced to the user. Chat is moderated with a visible reasoning trace — sensitive content like phone numbers and addresses auto-blur, reversibly. Safe Date is the offline loop: emergency contact, timed check-ins, live route, SOS — a first-class flow, not a settings page.

**Market & Business (1:05–1:35)**
> India's dating TAM is $2.5B by 2028. 2.5M+ LGBTQIA+ digital natives are underserved by current apps. Privacy-led products in India see 6× the willingness-to-pay of generic categories. Our model: free for adoption — trust score, dual identity, Safe Date all free; ₹499 Plus, ₹999 Premium, ₹1999 Elite for power features — boost, incognito, advanced filters, LLM starters. We charge for *power*, never for safety. Target ARPU ₹600/mo on paying tiers, 6–8% paying conversion, CAC payback under four months.

**Moat & Vision (1:35–1:50)**
> The moat is structural. Pehchaan Layer plus visible AI safety plus Safe Date plus audit infrastructure is a 12–18 month rebuild for any incumbent — and it cannibalizes their existing product. We start clean. The wedge is India's queer dating market. The prize is becoming the trust infrastructure layer for *every* product where identity is sensitive — fintech, healthcare, hiring, mental health, creators.

**Ask (1:50–2:00)**
> We're raising a seed round to ship the India web launch in Q1 2026, build out the AI safety stack, and follow with mobile. Trust is the next dating moat — Elyra is building it. I'd love to send you the deck and a five-minute interactive demo you can run yourself.

*Word count: ~430 · spoken in ~2 minutes at investor pace. Memorize the hook and the ask; the middle can flex by ±15 seconds.*
