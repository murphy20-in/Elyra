01 · Problem

Dating products assume everyone can be public.

For many LGBTQIA+ users in India, disclosure, harassment, fake profiles, and unsafe offline meetings are not edge cases. They are core adoption barriers.

Trust gap Users need identity control, moderation, verification, and safety workflows before they can comfortably connect.

Elyra is a trust operating system for modern connection.
